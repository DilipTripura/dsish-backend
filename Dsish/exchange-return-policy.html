Exchange & Refund Policy

Please read our policy carefully before submitting an exchange or refund request.

Exchange Policy

  • Products can be exchanged within 3 business days of delivery.
  • The item must be unused and in original packaging.
  • The product must not be damaged by the customer.
  • Exchange is subject to product availability.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are issued only if the product cannot be exchanged.
  • Refund requests must be made within 3 business days of delivery.
  • The product must be returned in original condition.
  • Refund will be processed to the original payment method.

Non-Eligible Cases

  • Requests made after the allowed time period.
  • Products damaged due to misuse.
  • Items returned without original packaging.

How to Request

To request an exchange or refund, go back to the exchange page and submit your request with:

  • Your Order ID
  • Reason for exchange/refund
  • Product image (if required)
